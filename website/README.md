# stitchfoundry.app: website drafts (WEB-01)

Status: draft, 2026-10-23. **Not published.** Anything public is the owner's decision, so these
pages wait for approval before they go anywhere.

- `index.html`: the home page (what it does, features, machines and formats, questions).
- `download.html`: the downloads, and what to do the first time it opens.
- `style.css`: one hand-written stylesheet, light and dark, no scripts, readable at phone width.

The pictures are the README's screenshots (`../docs/images/`, already in `ASSETS.md`); a published
site copies them next to the pages.

## For the owner to decide

1. The headline and the wording throughout.
2. Where it is hosted (GitHub Pages already serves the web app; the site could sit at the
   domain's root with the app under `/app`).
3. Whether to show credit prices (they aren't on the page yet).
4. The machines table: brands are named only to say which files the app writes.
5. Whether to link to MakerFoundry before it opens.

Every claim on the pages is something the app does today, from `docs/embroidery.md`; the
pre-release note says designs haven't been test-sewn on every machine yet.
